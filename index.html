About

A2 Textile Connect is a textile trading company built around one product: polyester yarn. We're based in Silvassa and Panipat — two of India's established centers for the yarn trade — which keeps us close to both production and demand.

We work directly with manufacturers and buyers, and keep that relationship simple: reliable sourcing, straight communication, and quality that doesn't vary from batch to batch.

Right now, that means serving the domestic market. Growing beyond it is next.
